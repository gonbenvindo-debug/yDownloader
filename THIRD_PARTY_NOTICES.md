# Third-party libraries

The extension bundles the following libraries. Their license texts are copied into `extension/third-party-licenses/` when the extension is built.

- [YouTube.js](https://github.com/LuanRT/YouTube.js) 18.1.0 — MIT
- [GoogleVideo](https://github.com/LuanRT/GoogleVideo) 4.0.4 — MIT (SABR/UMP stream parsing)
- [@bufbuild/protobuf](https://github.com/bufbuild/protobuf-es) 2.16.0 — Apache-2.0 AND BSD-3-Clause
- [fflate](https://github.com/101arrowz/fflate) 0.8.3 — MIT
- [Meriyah](https://github.com/meriyah/meriyah) 7.4.0 — ISC
- [Mediabunny](https://github.com/Vanilagy/mediabunny) 1.61.3 — Mozilla Public License 2.0
- [quickjs-emscripten-core](https://github.com/justjake/quickjs-emscripten) 0.32.0 — MIT
- [@jitl/quickjs-wasmfile-release-sync](https://github.com/justjake/quickjs-emscripten) 0.32.0 — MIT
- [@jitl/quickjs-ffi-types](https://github.com/justjake/quickjs-emscripten) 0.32.0 — MIT

The protobuf package does not include its license file in the published npm archive. Its upstream license text is included at `third-party-licenses/bufbuild-protobuf-2.16.0.txt` and copied into the built extension.
